When the control plane records an election without adopting it, the leader it still names now reports that it refused to open the elected generation (`refused` in the replica report: the generation and the node it promised it to), and placement names it again at the next generation on the same set (`election_refused`). Before, writes to the shard waited for the pending-set timeout or a lease lapse, or for good with no pending set. Placement acts only on that report, at exactly the assignment's generation, and not during a fenced move. Under Raft the field needs metadata level 22; under Postgres it is migration 0040 (#1730).
