`publish_if` works on a stream shard that runs on Raft (`FELIX_SHARD_ENGINE=raft`). The leader's log decides the condition at the index the entry lands at, and followers copy what it stored: the record when it is the expected offset, a blank entry otherwise. So on Raft a refused `publish_if` consumes one offset, a gap readers skip, and `offset_mismatch` names the tail past it; on the default engine a refusal consumes none. A conditional publish on Raft carries one record, and either answer comes at the commit. The conditional entry has its own tag in Raft wire layout 6, as defence-in-depth: leaders replicate entries read back from their log, so it is not sent today, and a member on an older build would refuse it rather than store the record unconditionally (#2446).
