- A cache watch resuming on a Raft cache shard is now replayed the changes it missed, deletes included, as on the hybrid engine, rather than answered with `resnapshot` and every key's current value. A member keeps its latest applied changes while it serves a watch of the shard and for five minutes after (up to 4096, or 1 MiB, per shard); a resume on a member that kept nothing for the shard, from before the oldest kept change, or past a snapshot install or a restart still resnapshots (#3006).
