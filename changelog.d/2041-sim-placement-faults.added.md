The simulator's nemesis can act as an operator on placement (#2041, #419): it moves shards and cancels moves, drains brokers so their follower seats are replaced, removes brokers, abandons held shards' logs (stopping the broker they are assigned to first, as the documented procedure does), and stops a broker between its promotion and its open, alone or right after a seat replacement took the shard's leader. Every action goes through the control plane's operator API. Turn them on with `SimConfig::with_placement_faults` or `FELIX_SIM_PLACEMENT=1`; they are off by default and keep a seed's other faults where they were. The `ElectionSettles` double-kill exemption now leaves out a leader killed before it opened, counts a joining follower in the set, and ends at an abandon; operations on an abandoned shard that started before the abandon are not held to it (appends count as unknown, reads are dropped), and everything after it is checked as usual.
