The simulator's nemesis can act as an operator on placement (#2041, #419): it moves shards and cancels moves, drains brokers so their follower seats are replaced, removes brokers, abandons held shards' logs, and stops a broker between its promotion and its open, alone or right after a seat replacement took the shard's leader. Every action goes through the control plane's operator API. Turn them on with `SimConfig::with_placement_faults` or `FELIX_SIM_PLACEMENT=1`; they are off by default and keep a seed's other faults where they were. The `ElectionSettles` double-kill exemption now leaves out a leader killed before it opened, counts a joining follower in the set, and ends at an abandon; abandoned shards are left out of the history check and `AckedHeldByLeader`.
