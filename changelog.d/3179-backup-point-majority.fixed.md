`felixctl backup-point` takes a Raft shard's point only when a majority of each of the shard's voter sets answered in the same round (both sets during a membership change). Before, a deposed leader that answered alone could be read as current, and a restore from that point dropped every write the newer leader acknowledged. A shard short of a majority is asked again, and then the command fails naming it. `/backup/offsets` now lists each Raft member's id, voter sets and the entry its membership is from.
