`felixctl raft reform plan [--scope cluster|node <id>]` prints a read-only re-form plan for Raft groups that lost their majority: per shard the copy a re-form would start from, the new voters, and whether acknowledged records could be lost. The control plane's metadata Raft leader holds the plan (`POST /v1/raft/reform/plans`, `GET /v1/raft/reform/plans/{id}`) and surveys the brokers, which answer with their node credential. Nothing is written. Brokers now also send the group epoch with a Raft copy id report (#2882).
