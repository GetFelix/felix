- MQTT subscribe on one broker (#1692). A device can subscribe to topics under a mounted stream, with `+` and `#`, at QoS 0 and 1 in a clean session, and is sent matching messages with `PUBACK` handling and a `Receive Maximum` bound. A slow device delays only itself. Reads are bounded by what the shard has committed, on the default engine and on Raft shards. Persistent sessions, retained messages, QoS 2 and reading shards owned by other brokers are not built.
