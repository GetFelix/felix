The broker raises its soft open-file limit to the hard limit at start. Each held shard keeps five files open, so the usual soft limit of 1,024 stopped a broker near 200 shards with "Too many open files". A broker whose limit holds fewer than 2,000 shards logs a warning with the number it does hold, and the new gauge `felix_broker_open_file_limit` reports the limit, and the sealed segments a broker keeps open are one cache for all its logs, sized by `FELIX_DURABLE_MAX_OPEN_SEALED_SEGMENTS` (default 1,024, at most a quarter of the open-file limit; a slot costs a descriptor and up to about 1 MiB of index memory) (#2351).
