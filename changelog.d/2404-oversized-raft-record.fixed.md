On Raft, an append whose first record cannot cross a 1 Gbit/s link within the heartbeat now gets the deadline that record needs, so one large record no longer times out on every retry and holds back every entry after it. A record over 32 MiB, which no append can carry, is refused before it is proposed with nothing written: an invalid, non-retryable error on the client protocol and `PUBACK` 0x83 `record_too_large` on MQTT (#2404).
