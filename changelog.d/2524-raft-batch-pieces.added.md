- A Raft stream shard's log now records each record's place in its idempotent batch, and a batch cut short by a leader change is finished by its re-send instead of being written again (#2524).
  Storage format v9 gains the batch piece record (bits 31 and 30, a 24-byte tag) and the `producers` snapshot moves to version 3, which keeps cut batches; versions 1 and 2 are still read. A re-send of a batch the log cannot account for (its first record purged while later ones are still in the log, or a record missing in the middle) is refused with `sequence_expired`. New idempotent batches of more than one record are still refused on a Raft shard.
