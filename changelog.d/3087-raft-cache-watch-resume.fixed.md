- A cache watch on a Raft cache shard that resumes on another member, such as the new leader after a failover, is now replayed from that member's recent changes instead of being resnapshotted. A member serving a watch has the shard's leader append a small watched entry, at most about one every five minutes per watched shard, and every member that applies it records the shard's changes for ten minutes past it. An unwatched shard appends none and records nothing. A resume from before where a member started recording is still resnapshotted (#3087).
