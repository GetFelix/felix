- A fenced shard move whose destination broker goes down no longer holds the shard's writes until the two-minute fence timeout. The pass after the broker is marked down gives the move up and the old leader serves again at a new generation, as an operator's cancel does (#1808).
- Placement no longer moves shards for balance while a broker is down or a restore is copying a shard back. Fencing a cache or stream then could refuse its writes for minutes while the remaining brokers took over the dead one's shards. Failover, restores, drains and an operator's moves are not held, and a broker that never returns holds balancing for at most `FELIX_SHARD_RESTORE_AFTER_MS` (#1808).
