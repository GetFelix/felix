Replicas elect a new leader themselves when a leader dies while the control plane is down (#1009). A follower that suspects its leader asks the set for a pre-vote, waits its place, writes its ballot and fences the set as a candidate; the other replicas then route to it, and the control plane adopts it when it returns. It runs only on brokers with `FELIX_INTERNAL_ELECTION` on, which now report `peer_election` and `replica_election`, once the fleet finalizes `replica_election` (after `joint_fence`). A shard leader now refuses a pre-vote against itself (#1815).
