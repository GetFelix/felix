`subscribe` takes an optional `end` offset: the subscription delivers a durable stream shard's records from its start up to `end` and finishes with `subscription_ended`, on the binary event path. A range below the committed tail is read from disk and never joins fanout; one above it follows live delivery and cuts at `end`. Negotiated with the extended feature bit `FEATURE_SUBSCRIBE_END` (`0x40` in the second word), so an older broker is never sent the field. Rust: `Connection::subscribe_range`, `Client::subscribe_range`, `Subscription::reached_end` (#1053).
