Metadata Raft: a member that lost its volume no longer answers a leader of an older term. It forgot the terms it promised, so a leader the group had replaced could commit with it and lose an acknowledged write. It now answers no leader until every other member has reported its term, and then only a leader at or above the highest. Until then it counts toward nothing, so with one of three members wiped, writes need both others. A member that is withheld too answers that it is, and counts as answered, so two wiped members do not wait on each other. `standing` reports the member's term, or `withheld` (#2365).
