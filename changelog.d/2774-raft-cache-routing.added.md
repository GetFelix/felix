A broker that runs Raft shards runs its durable cache shards on Raft groups too: puts, gets, conditional writes, deletes and counters, local or forwarded, are served by the shard's group with the same answers as the default engine, and survive the loss of the leader. A cache's `replication_factor` now reaches the broker from the control plane. A keyed cache watch is not served on a Raft cache shard yet (#2774).
