A promoted `Quorum` cache leader no longer opens on the lease because a replica cannot be reached (#1943). In a fleet that has finalized `majority_ack` but not `fenced_caches`, a promoted cache leader that could not learn a crashed replica's capabilities treated it as a broker without the fence and opened at once, skipping the catch-up, and a put the old leader had acknowledged could read as missing. Now only a replica that answers without the fence keeps a cache shard on the lease. An unreachable one counts as silent, and the fence needs a majority of the whole set, as it does without the lease. Stream shards are unchanged. Found by the deterministic simulator.
