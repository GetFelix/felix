A promoted shard leader no longer opens on the lease because a replica cannot be reached (#1943). In a fleet that has finalized `majority_ack` but not `fenced_caches`, a promoted `Quorum` cache leader that could not learn a crashed replica's capabilities treated it as a broker without the fence and opened at once, skipping the catch-up. If the promoted broker was behind, a put the old leader had acknowledged on the other replicas read as missing and could be overwritten. Now only a replica that answers without the fence keeps the lease. An unreachable one counts as silent, and the fence needs a majority of the whole set, as it does without the lease. A promoted stream leader in a fleet without `majority_ack` follows the same rule, so it now waits for a majority instead of opening on the lease. Found by the deterministic simulator.
