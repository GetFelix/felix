- The Python, TypeScript, C and C# clients take the reconnect backoff and max backoff, as the Rust client always has, with the Rust defaults (200 ms and 2 s) when unset: Python `reconnect_backoff` and `reconnect_max_backoff` in seconds, TypeScript `reconnectBackoff` and `reconnectMaxBackoff` in milliseconds, C `reconnect_backoff_ms` and `reconnect_max_backoff_ms`, C# `ReconnectBackoff` and `ReconnectMaxBackoff`. A max backoff below the backoff in effect is refused. `FelixConnectOptions` grew two fields after `reconnect_deadline_ms`; callers built against the older header keep the defaults.
