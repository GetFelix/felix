- A Raft log on a `periodic` or `none` broker whose run rotates its storage id after a crash can count an entry once it is written: the copy's `raft-reserved` is raised and synced first, and `flushed` and the log mark still wait for the fsync. A broker opens its Raft shards that way only once the fleet has finalized the new `raft_written_commit` feature, which no build reports yet. A snapshot syncs such a log before it is stored, a failed fsync wakes the flushes waiting on it, and every Raft log open fails on a damaged `raft-reserved`.
