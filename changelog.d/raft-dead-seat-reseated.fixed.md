- A Raft shard whose new member's broker died before it opened a copy is no longer stuck short of a copy. No target is stored until every member reports a copy, so the swap or top-up that seated that broker never reached the group and placement waited on it for good. Once the broker is lost, its seat now goes to another live broker, provided the group has formed and its reported voters do not name the broker. The write still has to keep a majority of the reported voters (#3310).
