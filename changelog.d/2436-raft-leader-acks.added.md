- On a Raft shard (`FELIX_SHARD_ENGINE=raft`) a publish to a `Leader` stream is acknowledged once it is on the leader's log, while the group's leader lease holds (#2436). Outside the lease it waits for its commit, as every publish to a `Quorum` stream does. Subscribers get the record at its commit either way. A record acknowledged this way is lost if the leader is replaced before it commits, which is the `Leader` level's contract. `FELIX_SHARD_RAFT_LEASE_MS` (1500 by default) sets the lease; `0` turns the early acknowledgement off, for hosts whose VMs can be paused. `raft::RaftEngine::publish` takes an `AnswerAt`, and `felix_broker::ProposedPublish::answer_at` answers a publisher before its batch commits.
