The control plane can now build the re-form planner's record for a Raft shard from the shard's record: its stream incarnation, every broker placed in the epoch, every target ordered in it, and the floor and offset floor its leaders reported at that incarnation (`ShardAssignment::reform_record`). A floor from another incarnation is dropped, and a group past epoch 0 gets no record until re-forms write their bases. The epoch's seed voters are not recorded yet, so every plan stays marked. Nothing calls it until the re-form command lands (#2250).
