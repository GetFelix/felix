- A broker bounds how many of its per-shard Raft groups are in an election at once, so groups that all lose their leader together no longer set off round after round of vote fsyncs on a slow disk (#2317). The rest wait their turn. The bound falls only while the broker's vote saves are slow, and groups with no quorum in reach do not hold the others back. Behind the `raft` feature, which no release enables.
