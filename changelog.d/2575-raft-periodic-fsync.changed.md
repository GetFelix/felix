- A Raft shard on a `periodic` or `none` broker no longer fsyncs its log on every commit. Every build now reports the `raft_written_commit` fleet feature and a new cluster finalizes it, so the log counts an entry once it is written and covered by the copy's reservation, and fsyncs on the broker's periodic schedule like the default engine. Votes and terms are still synced before a vote is answered. An upgraded cluster keeps fsyncing on commit until an operator finalizes the feature, and once finalized no broker may be downgraded to a build without the reservation file.
