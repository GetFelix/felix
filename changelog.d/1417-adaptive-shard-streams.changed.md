**Breaking config change:** `FELIX_PUB_SHARD_STREAMS` means something else, and the fixed per-shard publish streams are gone. The Rust client used to give the first shards it saw (4 per broker by default) a publish stream each, up front. Now it keeps every shard on its pooled publish streams and moves a shard onto a stream of its own only while an answer at the head of its stream is overdue (eight times the stream's usual wait, at least 250 ms). The other shards of that stream move too rather than queue behind it. A move waits for the shard's publishes in flight to be answered, so per-shard order holds, and a moved shard returns to the pool once it and the pool have been calm for 5 s (longer if it keeps stalling, up to 5 minutes). `FELIX_PUB_SHARD_STREAMS` is now the most shards moved at once per broker, default 64; `0` still keeps every shard on the pool. A stalled shard no longer keeps timing out its stream-mates during a failover (#1417, #1920).
