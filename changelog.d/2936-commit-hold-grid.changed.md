- A Raft leader holds the append that carries only a new commit index for 20 to 40 ms (`RuntimeConfig::commit_hold`, now 20 ms, was 1 ms) on a grid every group on the broker shares, so a write that comes in that time carries the commit instead, and the held appends of many groups to one follower go in one heartbeat batch. A heartbeat to the follower takes a held append along; a read on a follower learns the commit from its read-index round's heartbeat and does not wait. On a quiet stream, followers and their subscribers see a lone record up to 41 ms after the leader does. The heartbeat window's opener now sends a batch of one itself, with no task (#2936).
