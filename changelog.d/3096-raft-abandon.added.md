- Raft: `POST /v1/placement/abandon` gives up the log of a Raft shard whose every copy is gone. It is one `AbandonRaftEpoch` command (metadata level 56), refused until every broker placed in the group epoch is declared dead in the store and the fleet finalizes `raft_abandon`, and refused until the restore time line is a full 2^32 gap above the offset floor and every earlier epoch's first index (about four and a half minutes after that epoch began; the control-plane clocks must agree within about four minutes). The new leader starts the shard empty at a recorded first index above every offset the lost group gave out; no broker forms a group past epoch 0 from index 0. (#3096)
