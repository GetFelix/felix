- Raft: `POST /v1/placement/abandon` gives up the log of a Raft shard whose every copy is gone. It is one `AbandonRaftEpoch` command (metadata level 56), refused until every broker placed in the group epoch is declared dead and the fleet finalizes `raft_abandon`. The new leader starts the shard empty at a recorded first index above every offset the lost group gave out; no broker forms a group past epoch 0 from index 0. (#3096)
