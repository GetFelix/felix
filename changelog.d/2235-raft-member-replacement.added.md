- Raft shards now change their members when a copy has to move. A drained broker's follower copies, and those of a broker gone for `restore_after_millis`, are swapped for another broker one member at a time, and an operator move to a broker outside the set gives it the leader's seat (step `raft_replace`) instead of being refused with `not_a_member`. The group's leader adds the new copy as a learner, waits for it to catch up and changes the voters through joint consensus, handing its leadership on first when its own seat moves. A move outside the set is refused with `member_down` or `set_unsettled` while the group cannot take it, and with `single_member` for a one-member group. The placement step `raft_replace_internal` is now `raft_replace` (#2235). A set change now keeps the shard's Raft target counter (metadata level 50); it used to start again at 1, so the next target was no newer than the group's membership and its leader never acted on it.
