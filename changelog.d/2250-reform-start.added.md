The storage side of a Raft re-form's start: `raft_copy::start_copy` starts a voter at its base with a purged id, snapshot id and saved commit at the end of the gap, empties a learner holding records past the base, reserves through the gap and checks both promises the vouch rule needs before it finishes (#2640). It runs under a `reform-building` marker that keeps the copy from opening until it completes. A copy started this way reads as its base on the next re-form, and the plan's first index stays above the shard's reported `offset_floor`. Nothing calls it yet (#2250).
