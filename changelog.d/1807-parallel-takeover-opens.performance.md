After a failover, a broker opens the shards it takes over up to 8 at a time instead of one after another, with the epoch-file and log-open fsyncs on the blocking pool. Each shard serves as soon as its own open (and fence) is done, instead of after every shard in the pass has opened. A shard the pass still has work for keeps its previous route, so replication never sees this broker named leader of a shard it has not opened (#1807).
