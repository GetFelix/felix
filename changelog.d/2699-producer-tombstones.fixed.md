An idempotent producer whose batches retention or a Raft purge removed altogether is no longer forgotten outright: its newest sequence is kept as a tombstone (up to 4096 per shard, in `producers.forgotten` and in a stream shard's snapshot data), so a re-send of its first batch is refused with `sequence_expired` instead of being written again. A build without tombstones refuses to open a Raft log whose `producers.forgotten` has them, and refuses to install a snapshot that carries them (#2699).
