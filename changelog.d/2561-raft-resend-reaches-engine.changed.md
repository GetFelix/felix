- A re-send of an idempotent batch of several records on a Raft shard now reaches the shard's log, through a client's publish and through a forward between brokers. One the log holds whole is answered as a duplicate, and one a leader change cut short is finished and answered from its first record's original offset. A new batch of several records is still refused with `invalid_request`, now at the producer's turn rather than before it. A forwarded batch is answered with its last record's real offset, which can be past its first plus its length when other records lie between them.
