- The Raft engine has its leader lease (#2249). A `Leader` write is acknowledged on the leader's log while a majority answered within the lease (1.5 s) and waits for its commit otherwise; the publish path does not use it yet (#2436). A member gives the lease up before openraft sees a message that ends its term, a transfer gives it up before it is asked for, a suspended broker refuses for one lease after it wakes, and a restarted member that may have promised a leader takes no vote and does not stand for one election timeout. A restarted leader and a sole voter promised nobody: they are not held, and the leader stands again as it starts. The config refuses a lease that could outlast a follower's promise under the clock drift bound. Behind the `raft` feature.
