- `felix_storage::raft_copy` reads what a re-form needs from a broker's copies of a shard without opening them: the first index a new epoch may give out, above the base, the stored offset floor and every `raft-reserved` in the shard directory and its kept directories, and for each kept directory the commit it can vouch for, its saved commit cut to its last synced entry the way an open cuts it. Nothing calls them until the re-form command is built, and `raft_written_commit` stays unreported until then.
