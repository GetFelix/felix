- The MQTT listener publishes at QoS 0 and 1 to a mounted durable stream whose shard the broker owns (#1691). A `PUBACK` is sent only after the write has the acknowledgement the stream's consistency asks for, in the order the publishes arrived, and a write whose outcome is unknown closes the connection instead of answering. Each connection publishes under its own producer id, batches publishes read while a write is out, and is held to `FELIX_MQTT_MAX_INFLIGHT` (default 64), advertised as `Receive Maximum`. The topic is the record's key and the MQTT fields are `felix.mqtt.*` headers, so the first MQTT publish to a mounted stream moves its logs to storage format v8. A shard owned by another broker is refused, since forwarding comes later; QoS 2 and retained publishes are refused. Counted in `felix_mqtt_published_total` and `felix_mqtt_refused_total{reason}`.
