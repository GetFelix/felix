The tail caches of a broker's Raft logs share one budget of 256 MiB (`RuntimeConfig::tail_cache_bytes`), on top of the 4 MiB each log already kept. Logs share it evenly when many hold bytes, and when the total is still over, the logs appended to least recently give their caches up first, so thousands of idle groups no longer hold 4 MiB each. A dropped cache is read from the segments. The new `felix_broker_raft_tail_cache_bytes` gauge reports the bytes held (#2339).
