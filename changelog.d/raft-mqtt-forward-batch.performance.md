- MQTT on a Raft shard: a broker that is a member of the shard's group but does not lead it forwards a connection's batch to the leader in one forward instead of one message at a time, and the leader proposes the messages back to back. This was the main cost of MQTT publish on Raft (about a third of the hybrid's throughput with most publishes forwarded). The forward is a new internal kind, `RecordSequencedForwardPublish`, sent only to a leader that offers the `RECORD_SEQUENCED_FORWARDS` peer capability; older peers get one forward per message as before.
