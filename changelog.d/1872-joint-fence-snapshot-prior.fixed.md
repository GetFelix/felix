Under `joint_fence`, a leader that learned its shard from a control-plane snapshot no longer waits forever when it cannot know the replica set its generation replaced. That happened to a leader restarted after the finalize at a generation that started before it, and to a new shard whose first assignment reached its leader in a snapshot. The leader now reports the generation refused with no node named, and placement names it again one generation up on the same set (step `set_unknown`), where the set being left is known (#1872).
