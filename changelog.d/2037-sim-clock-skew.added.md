felix-sim can give each broker and the control plane its own clocks: a lease clock with its own rate and a wall clock with its own offset, drawn from the seed and stepped or re-skewed mid-run (#2037). `FELIX_SIM_CLOCKS=bounded` stays within the drift and skew the docs say Felix tolerates; `unbounded` goes past them. Off by default, and it needs the `tokio_unstable` build `task sim:replay` uses. `felix_common::clock::sim::install_wall_offset` lets a simulator move wall readings apart from the lease clock.
