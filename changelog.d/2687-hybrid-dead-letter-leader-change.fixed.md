A consumer group no longer hands out a dead-lettered record again after a leader change that lands before the cursor advances over it. A rebuilt group settles every dead letter at or above its cursor, and a seek clears the dead letters at or above its new position so they are delivered again, on hybrid and Raft shards alike (#2687). On a hybrid shard the clear counts only once the seek's cursor is written: a seek that crashes, fails or is cancelled part way leaves every dead letter listed and redrivable and the group where it was. The Raft seek fold change has no version gate, because Raft groups are unreleased and 0.8 starts fresh: a rolling restart of a preview Raft cluster across this change would diverge.
