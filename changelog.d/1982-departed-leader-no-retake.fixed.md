A fenced shard is no longer handed back to a leader that has deregistered. When a move off such a broker gave up (the destination died or the fence timed out), or no other copy could take the shard after the leader drained, placement gave the shard back to the departing broker at a new generation, which it might not live to serve. The shard now stays fenced until another caught-up copy can take it or the broker's lease runs out and it fails over (#1982).
