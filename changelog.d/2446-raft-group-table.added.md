Every member of a Raft stream shard now folds the shard's consumer-group writes into one group table (cursor, dead letters and redriven records per group), keeps it across a restart, and carries it in the stream snapshot, so a member that installs a snapshot holds the same table as one that applied every entry. Consumer groups on a Raft shard are still refused until the fleet finalizes `raft_consumer_groups` (#2446).
