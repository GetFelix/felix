`felixctl raft reform plan --restore-point FILE` re-forms each Raft shard from a backup point: a shard whose base does not end at the point's offset, or that the point does not name at its incarnation, is blocked with the reason, and the next epoch starts at least an offset gap past the point (2^40 by default, `--offset-gap N`, below 2^32 only with `--accept-short-gap`), so offsets acknowledged after the point are not given out again. `POST /v1/raft/reform/plans` takes the same as `restore`.
