A failed sync no longer goes unrecorded outside a log's own files. A failed sync of a node record under the durable root (copy trust, the registration and other markers, the control-plane view, the stream incarnation markers) stops every later durable record in that directory until the broker restarts, counts as a poisoned log, and is named on `/ready`. A failed sync of an offloaded copy, or after removing a retired shard's copy, stops offloading to that archive. A flush whose caller stopped waiting still poisons its log, and a crash after the poisoned-log cut but before the durable mark was rewritten now has the mark rewritten on the next start (#2309, #2299, #2483, #2357).
