`trim_before` works on a stream shard that runs on Raft (`FELIX_SHARD_ENGINE=raft`), answered as on the default engine. The trim is one Raft entry, conditional on landing at `expected_offset`; every member applies its floor from the log once it commits, after a restart too, and writes the `head-trimmed` marker before storing it. A Raft log deletes nothing below a floor until openraft purges past it. The entry has its own tag (6), which an older build refuses, so a cluster accepts Raft trims only once it finalizes the new fleet feature `raft_head_trim` (needs `head_trim`); until then a trim on a Raft shard is refused with `feature_not_enabled`. A trim refused at its append consumes one offset, as a refused `publish_if` does on Raft (#2446).
