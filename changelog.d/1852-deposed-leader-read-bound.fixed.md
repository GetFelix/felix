A leader that lost a `Quorum` stream to a move no longer answers reads for it as if it still served it (#1852). After a move to a route with no replicas it could deliver records to live subscribers before a majority had them, and history reads, Kafka `Fetch` and consumer-group polls could return them too. A Kafka `ListOffsets` for `latest`, or a group seek to `latest`, that raced the move could answer the start of the log, rewinding the consumer. The read bound now checks that this broker serves the shard before anything else. Where it does not, group operations are refused as fenced and Kafka answers `NOT_LEADER_OR_FOLLOWER`. A broker that serves a shard with no replicas still reads it in full.
