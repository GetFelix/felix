A leader that lost a `Quorum` stream to a move onto a route with no replicas no longer treats the stream as fully committed (#1852). It could deliver records to live subscribers before a majority had them, and history reads, Kafka `Fetch` and consumer-group polls could return them too. The read bound now checks that this broker serves the shard before anything else, and hands out nothing past the mark where it does not. A broker that serves a shard with no replicas still reads it in full.
