A leader deposed by a move of a `Quorum` stream to a route with no replicas no longer treats the stream as unbounded (#1852). It answered its readers as if every record were committed, so its release task delivered batches it held for the mark and a history read went past the mark. The committed bound now checks that this broker serves the shard before anything else, and is `Refused` where it does not. A broker that serves a shard with no replicas still reads it unbounded.
