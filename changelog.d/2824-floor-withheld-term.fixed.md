- Metadata Raft: a wiped member's floor now takes the term a withheld peer follows. Before, under a `waive-lost` waiver after two wipes in three, the floor could come from a deposed leader alone, and that leader could then acknowledge a write the next leader dropped. A withheld member reports its term in a new `standing` field, `withheld_term`; past the failure tolerance, a withheld peer on an older build that does not report it is waited on (#2824).
