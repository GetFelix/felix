A follower fenced for a candidate's ballot refused every batch from its leader for the rest of the generation, so the shard ran one copy short, with one less failure to spare. If the candidate withdrew, nothing renamed the shard, so that copy never rejoined. The leader now reports its generation refused once the follower's ballot has lasted 10 s, carrying that ballot, and the control plane names the same leader past it, so the follower takes batches again. A leader left without a majority by such ballots still reports at once, but carries the ballot only after the same 10 s, and carries its own copy's ballot instead when that one is higher (#1949).
