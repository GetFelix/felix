- On Raft, placement now adds members to a shard's set that is short of its replication factor, one live broker outside the set per write, until the set is back at the factor. Before, nothing grew such a set, so a shard an abandon left on one copy, or one placed while too few brokers were live, kept acknowledging `Quorum` writes on fewer copies than its factor for good. The step is `raft_top_up`.
- On Raft, `felix_shards_under_replicated` and `GET /v1/placement/replication` count only the copies a shard's group reports voting, so a learner that never catches up no longer hides a shard short of its factor.
