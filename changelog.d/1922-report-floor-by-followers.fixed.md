A `Quorum` leader acknowledging by its followers (`majority_ack`, and `fenced_caches` for caches) no longer reports a follower as a promotion candidate before it holds the log the leader inherited. Two failovers in a row with a spare broker swapped in could otherwise promote a broker that never held an acknowledged write and open without it. The same floor now applies to a cache's counter log, counted from the leader's current stint (#1922).
