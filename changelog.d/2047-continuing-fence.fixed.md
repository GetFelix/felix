- A leader that keeps serving into its next generation no longer stays closed when that generation re-admits a member after two members crashed uncleanly (#2047). Under `joint_fence` its fence needs only promises: it counts an answer from any copy and takes no tail. A copy doubted after an unclean crash answers it when its ballot names that leader at the generation before and the crash kept every ballot; a wiped or restored directory, or one that already replaced a lost copy, still refuses. A failover after the same two crashes still waits for `felixctl placement abandon`.
